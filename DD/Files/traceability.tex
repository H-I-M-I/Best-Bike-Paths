This section relates the functional requirements defined in the RASD (IDs \texttt{RE-F*})
to the main DD artifacts that realize them: (i) architectural components and their responsibilities,
(ii) component interfaces, (iii) runtime interaction diagrams, and (iv) user interface elements.
The purpose is to demonstrate that each requirement is supported by concrete design choices and
a clear end-to-end execution path.

%-------------------- F2 --------------------
\multicolumn{3}{|l|}{\textbf{F2 - Trip Recording and Review}}\\
\hline
RE-F2-1 &
The system shall allow users to record a biking trip. &
\textbf{Components:} Mobile App, Trip Management, Backend API, Database.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Trip recording and upload flow.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Trip recording screen.\\
\hline

RE-F2-2 &
The system shall store recorded trips for later access. &
\textbf{Components:} Trip Management, Database.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Persistent storage after trip completion.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Trip history and summary screens.\\
\hline

RE-F2-3 &
The system shall acquire GPS data during a biking session. &
\textbf{Components:} Mobile App, GPS sensor services.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Continuous GPS acquisition during trip recording.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Live position feedback during recording.\\
\hline

RE-F2-4 &
The system shall acquire motion sensor data during a biking session. &
\textbf{Components:} Mobile App, Motion sensors.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Motion data sampling during recording.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Background acquisition indicators.\\
\hline

RE-F2-5 &
The system shall associate recorded trips with the corresponding user. &
\textbf{Components:} Authentication service, Trip Management.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} User identity attached to trip metadata.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} User-specific trip lists.\\
\hline

RE-F2-7 &
The system shall enrich recorded trips with contextual information when available. &
\textbf{Components:} Trip Management, Weather Information Service.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Contextual enrichment after trip upload.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Contextual data displayed in trip details.\\
\hline

RE-F2-8 &
The system shall allow users to review previously recorded trips. &
\textbf{Components:} Trip Management, Backend API, Database.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Trip retrieval flow.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Trip history and detail views.\\
\hline


%-------------------- F3 --------------------
\multicolumn{3}{|l|}{\textbf{F3 - Manual Contribution}}\\
\hline
RE-F3-1 &
The system shall allow users to manually contribute information about path segments. &
\textbf{Components:} Path Contribution logic, Backend API, Database.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Manual contribution submission flow.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Path contribution screen.\\
\hline

RE-F3-2 &
The system shall allow users to specify the condition or status of path segments. &
\textbf{Components:} Path Contribution logic, Aggregation service.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Validation and storage of segment status.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Segment status selection controls.\\
\hline

RE-F3-3 &
The system shall allow users to publish contributed path information. &
\textbf{Components:} Aggregation and Moderation service.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Publication after validation.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Submit and confirmation actions.\\
\hline

RE-F3-4 &
The system shall allow users to modify or remove previously submitted contributions. &
\textbf{Components:} Path Contribution logic, Database.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Update and deletion flows.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Contribution management interface.\\
\hline


%-------------------- F4 --------------------
\multicolumn{3}{|l|}{\textbf{F4 - Issue Detection and Validation}}\\
\hline
RE-F4-1 &
The system shall automatically detect potential issues along recorded trips. &
\textbf{Components:} Issue Detection service, Mobile App.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Automatic detection after data upload.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Issue notifications.\\
\hline

RE-F4-2 &
The system shall present detected issues to users for validation. &
\textbf{Components:} Issue Validation service, Backend API.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Retrieval of candidate issues.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Issue validation screen.\\
\hline

RE-F4-3 &
The system shall allow users to confirm detected issues. &
\textbf{Components:} Issue Validation service, Aggregation service.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Confirmation flow updating issue state.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Confirm controls.\\
\hline

RE-F4-4 &
The system shall allow users to reject detected issues. &
\textbf{Components:} Issue Validation service.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Rejection and storage of decision.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Reject controls.\\
\hline

RE-F4-5 &
The system shall ensure that only validated issues are exposed to other users. &
\textbf{Components:} Aggregation service, Path Search service.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Filtering of non-validated issues.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Map markers for validated issues only.\\
\hline


%-------------------- F5 --------------------
\multicolumn{3}{|l|}{\textbf{F5 - Path Search and Visualization}}\\
\hline
RE-F5-1 &
The system shall allow users to search for biking paths between two locations. &
\textbf{Components:} Path Search service, Routing services.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Path search execution.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Search controls on the main map.\\
\hline

RE-F5-2 &
The system shall compute feasible biking paths between origin and destination. &
\textbf{Components:} Path Search service, Aggregation service.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Routing and path computation flow.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Map-based path visualization.\\
\hline

RE-F5-3 &
The system shall rank computed paths according to quality indicators. &
\textbf{Components:} Aggregation service, Path Search service.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Path scoring and ranking.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Ranked list of paths.\\
\hline

RE-F5-4 &
The system shall allow users to visualize suggested biking paths. &
\textbf{Components:} Client UI, Path Search service.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Path retrieval and rendering.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Main map view with overlays.\\
\hline

RE-F5-5 &
The system shall allow users to view detailed information about suggested paths. &
\textbf{Components:} Path Search service, Database.\\
& & \textbf{Runtime:} Retrieval of path details.\\
& & \textbf{UI:} Path detail screen with legend.\\
\hline

\end{longtable}
